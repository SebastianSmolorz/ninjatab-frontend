Four friends, two weeks in China, five cities and everything between them by train. The Great
Wall at Mutianyu, the Terracotta Army in Xi'an, four days on the cliffs at Zhangjiajie, hotpot
and pandas in Chengdu, then the Bund in Shanghai. Hostels, high-speed rail, an overnight
sleeper and a lot of street food. Here is that itinerary costed out in full, with every
shared expense itemised in Ninja Tab so you can see who pays for what and what everyone owes
at the end.

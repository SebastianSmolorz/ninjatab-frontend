A five day ski trip to Tatranská Lomnica in Slovakia cost five friends £2,173 in February 2026,
about £435 each. Flights from Bristol were £98 each, four nights in a hotel £86 each, and the
two of us who skied paid €161 for a ski pass and £68 for gear rental on top.

We went from 10 to 14 February 2026, flying from Bristol to Poprad-Tatry airport, a short taxi
journey from the ski slopes, and staying four nights in Tatranská Lomnica, a resort village in
Slovakia's High Tatras. We mostly got around on the tram between the ski slopes and the nearby
town, and the rest of the days went on a trip to Aquacity, Slovak pubs and a lot of cheese. We
tracked all 29 shared expenses in Ninja Tab along the way. Here's the full Tatranská Lomnica ski
trip cost breakdown, including who paid for what and what everyone owed at the end.

A four day trip to Sardinia cost four friends £2,166 in June 2026, about £541 each. The Airbnb was
£512 in total, four days of scooter hire £80.50 each, and a rented boat out to Tavolara about €58
each with fuel and docking. Two of us also logged flights, at £118 each.

We flew to Olbia and got around on the scooters, with beach bars, pizza in Murta Maria and a lot of
Ichnusa, aperol and gelato along the way. One day went on the boat, looking for wild dolphins and
stopping at Tavolara island, and on the last day we paid €12 each to leave our bags at Olbia
airport. It could have been much cheaper with taxis and fewer drinks, but the scooters were worth
it and a boat is essential on the Mediterranean. We tracked all 41 shared expenses in Ninja Tab
along the way. Here's the full Sardinia trip cost breakdown, including who paid for what and what
everyone owed at the end.

Two friends island hopping the Gulf of Thailand. A hostel and a Pig Island boat trip out of Koh
Samui, sundowners at Coco Tams, roast duck at 995 and a wander round Fisherman's Village, then
the boat over to Koh Tao for Ao Leuk beach, a zip line, a jungle party and a pub crawl, before
the long bus back to Bangkok. Twenty-one shared expenses, mostly paid in baht and settled up in
pounds, every one of them itemised in Ninja Tab so you can see who paid for what and what each
of them owed at the end.
